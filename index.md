## AI 101: what do I need to know first?

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

### What is AI, really?

**Today's AI tools are software trained on huge numbers of examples (text, images, audio) that produce new text, images or answers from the patterns they learned.** They do not 'know' things the way a person does; they are very good at producing likely, useful-looking output. The free Elements of AI course points out that AI means different things to different people, so it helps to be specific: most people today mean chatbots like ChatGPT or Claude.

Sources: [Elements of AI, chapter 1](https://course.elementsofai.com/1/1)

### What is a chatbot like ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

**A chat app that sits on top of a 'model', the trained AI that actually writes the replies.** You type or speak; the model answers. Companies release new models every few weeks, which is why the Latest models table below exists.

Sources: [Latest models table (lab sources)](https://aistarthere.github.io/#models)

### Can AI be wrong?

**Yes. AI can confidently invent facts, links or numbers that sound right.** Treat it like a fast, clever intern: great for drafts and ideas, but check anything important yourself, especially health, money and legal questions. Ask it for links and open them.

### How do I write a good prompt?

**Say who it should act as, what you want, who it is for and what format you need.** Example: 'Act as a friendly tutor. Explain inflation to a 14-year-old in 5 bullet points.' Then reply with 'shorter', 'more examples' or 'now as a table'.

### Is it safe to paste personal information into a chatbot?

**Don't paste passwords, bank details, medical records or other people's private information.** Check each app's settings for whether your chats are used for training. Claude's pricing page, for example, lists model training as opt-out on its consumer plans.

Sources: [Claude plans](https://claude.com/pricing)

### Do I need to pay to use AI?

**No. ChatGPT and Claude both have free plans, with usage limits.** ChatGPT's pricing page says the free version is available to everyone; Claude's Free plan is $0. Upgrade only when you keep hitting a limit doing something genuinely useful.

Sources: [ChatGPT plans](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude plans](https://claude.com/pricing)

### Which AI chatbot should a beginner start with?

**Start with the free plan of ChatGPT or Claude; both work on web and mobile.** Both pricing pages list free chat on the web and phone apps, plus voice. Pick one, use it for a week on real tasks (an email, a plan, a summary), then try the other on the same task and compare.

Sources: [ChatGPT plans](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude plans](https://claude.com/pricing)

Go deeper (free): [Elements of AI: free online course for non-experts (University of Helsinki & MinnaLearn)](https://www.elementsofai.com/), [ChatGPT plans (what is free)](https://chatgpt.com/pricing), [Claude plans (what is free)](https://claude.com/pricing)

## What's new in AI this week?

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

### Anthropic hands its 'Agent Skills' standard to the Agentic AI Foundation (2026-10-22)

Anthropic is donating Agent Skills, an open standard for teaching AI agents specific tasks, to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation. It says more than 70 agent platforms, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code, have adopted it since December 2025. Why it matters: Skills you set up in one AI tool are more likely to work in others. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation)

### OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud (2026-10-21)

OpenAI says its frontier models and Codex are coming to Google Cloud's Model Garden, bought through Google Cloud Marketplace. General availability is planned 'in the coming weeks'; Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner. Why it matters: Rival clouds are carrying each other's models, so businesses get more choice. [Source](https://openai.com/index/openai-models-google-cloud)

### Meta releases the weights of Muse Spark Open (2026-10-15)

Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date: 816B parameters (42B active), with a 1M-token context window. Why it matters: Anyone with the hardware can download and run it, rather than renting it through an app. [Source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-spark-open)

### Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 and cuts a Sonnet price (2026-10-07)

Anthropic says Haiku 5.5 costs around 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5. Sonnet 5.5 cache reads were also halved. Why it matters: Small, cheap models keep getting better, so AI features get cheaper to build. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)

### Mistral previews Mistral Large 4, trained in Europe (2026-10-06)

A 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model, trained in Mistral's own European datacenters. Weights are promised by the end of October. Why it matters: A strong European option for people and businesses who care where their AI runs. [Source](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4/)

## What can I try with AI today?

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

### Ask it to teach you something (ChatGPT (Free) - Study mode)

Prompt: "I want to understand how mortgages work in Ireland. Teach me step by step, ask me one question at a time, and check I understood before moving on."

Tip: ChatGPT's pricing page lists Study mode on the Free plan. Check anything Ireland-specific with an official source. [Source](https://chatgpt.com/pricing)

### Fix an email you're dreading (Any free chatbot)

Prompt: "Rewrite this email so it is polite but firm, under 120 words, and ends with one clear next step: [paste email]"

Tip: Ask for 3 versions, then say which one you like and why.

### Plan a week of dinners on a budget (Any free chatbot)

Prompt: "Plan 5 weeknight dinners for 2 adults, under 30 minutes each, using ingredients from an Irish supermarket. Give me one combined shopping list grouped by aisle."

Tip: Do not trust its prices. Use it for the plan and the list only.

### Have a spoken conversation (ChatGPT or Claude voice mode (Free, limited))

Prompt: "Let's practise a job interview for a [role]. Ask me one question at a time and give me feedback after each answer."

Tip: Both pricing pages list voice on their free plans (ChatGPT says 'limited'). [Source](https://claude.com/pricing)

### Build a tiny app without coding (Claude (Free) - Artifacts)

Prompt: "Make me a simple interactive tip calculator in euro that splits a bill between friends."

Tip: Claude's Free plan lists 'Create Artifacts'. Then try: 'add a dark mode'. [Source](https://claude.com/pricing)

### Make it show its sources (Claude (Free) - web search)

Prompt: "What changed in the latest AI model releases this month? Give me links to the official announcements for every claim."

Tip: Click the links. If one doesn't support the claim, don't use the claim. [Source](https://claude.com/pricing)

### Turn a photo into notes (ChatGPT (Free) - vision (limited))

Prompt: "Here's a photo of my whiteboard. Turn it into tidy meeting notes with action items and owners."

Tip: Never upload photos of IDs, bank cards or other people's private info. [Source](https://chatgpt.com/pricing)

## Which everyday tasks can I automate for free?

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

### Log important emails to a spreadsheet automatically

Level: Beginner. Time: 20 min. Tools: Zapier (Free), Gmail, Google Sheets.

1. Make a Google Sheet with columns: Date, From, Subject.
2. In Zapier, create a Zap. Trigger: Gmail 'New email matching search' (e.g. from:bank OR subject:invoice).
3. Action: Google Sheets 'Create spreadsheet row' and map Date, From and Subject.
4. Test it, then turn it on. Zapier's free plan allows two-step Zaps, 100 tasks a month and checks every 15 minutes.
5. If a step says 'Premium', it isn't on the free plan. Pick a different app.

Free-plan limits: https://zapier.com/pricing

### Collect feedback with a form and get a weekly AI summary

Level: Beginner. Time: 25 min. Tools: Tally (Free), Google Sheets, ChatGPT or Claude (Free).

1. Build a short feedback form in Tally (3 to 5 questions).
2. In the form's Integrations tab, connect Google Sheets. Every response becomes a new row (free on all Tally plans).
3. Once a week, copy the new rows and paste them into a chatbot with: 'Summarise the top 3 themes, the most common complaint, and one quick fix.'
4. Add the summary to a 'Weekly notes' tab so you can see trends.

Free-plan limits: https://tally.so/help/google-sheets-integration

### A reusable 'meeting notes to action items' assistant

Level: Beginner. Time: 15 min. Tools: Claude (Free) - Projects.

1. In Claude, create a Project called 'Meeting notes'. The Free plan allows up to 5 Projects.
2. Add these instructions: 'When I paste notes or a transcript, return: 1) 3-line summary, 2) decisions, 3) action items as a table (owner, task, due date), 4) open questions.'
3. After each meeting, paste your rough notes into a new chat inside the Project.
4. Copy the action-item table into your to-do app or email.

Free-plan limits: https://claude.com/pricing

### Build your own weekly news digest

Level: Intermediate. Time: 40 min. Tools: Make (Free), Google Sheets, Any free chatbot.

1. Pick 3 to 5 sites you trust that have an RSS feed.
2. In Make, create a scenario: an RSS 'watch feed items' module, then Google Sheets 'Add a row' (title, link, date). TODO: confirm the module names in Make's editor.
3. Schedule it to run once a day. Make's free plan gives 1,000 credits a month, and most actions use 1 credit.
4. On Fridays, paste the week's rows into a chatbot: 'Group these into themes and write a 5-bullet digest with the links.'

Free-plan limits: https://www.make.com/en/pricing

### A personal FAQ helper for your small business or club

Level: Beginner. Time: 30 min. Tools: ChatGPT (Free) - Projects.

1. Write your opening hours, prices, policies and common answers into one document.
2. In ChatGPT, create a Project (listed on the Free plan) and add the document.
3. Instruction: 'Answer customer questions using only this document. If the answer isn't there, say so and suggest they contact us.'
4. Paste a customer's question in, review the draft, then send it yourself. Don't let it reply unsupervised.

Free-plan limits: https://chatgpt.com/pricing

### Snap receipts into a simple expenses sheet

Level: Beginner. Time: 15 min. Tools: ChatGPT (Free, limited uploads), Google Sheets.

1. Create a Google Sheet with columns: Date, Shop, Total (EUR), Category.
2. Take photos of a few receipts and upload them to ChatGPT with: 'Extract date, shop, total in euro and a category for each. Output CSV with headers Date,Shop,Total,Category.'
3. Check every number against the receipt. AI misreads photos.
4. Paste the CSV into the sheet (Data > Split text to columns if needed) and add a SUM at the bottom.

Free-plan limits: https://chatgpt.com/pricing
